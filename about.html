About the creator

I'm Marvellous Udoko. Most people call me Marvforge. I'm a 300 level Geomatics Engineering student at the University of Benin, and I run things as Assistant General Secretary for the Anglican Students' Fellowship, UNIBEN/UBTH.

uniVERSE didn't start as some big idea. It started because I was tired. Tired of scrolling through a WhatsApp group from three sessions ago trying to find a past question that somebody definitely sent but nobody can locate anymore. Tired of missing fellowship updates because they got buried under fifty other messages about things that had nothing to do with me. Tired of being on a campus with thousands of people and still not knowing who around me actually cared about the same things I did.

So I built the thing I wished already existed.

I'm not doing this for a portfolio screenshot. I build tools for problems I'm actually stuck inside of, not problems that look good in a pitch deck. If I'm not living the problem myself, I probably shouldn't be the one solving it.

uniVERSE is still young. It breaks sometimes. Some parts are half built and I know exactly which ones. But it's real, it's running, and it gets better every week because I'm using it too, not just building it and walking away.

If something's broken, or missing, or just annoying you, tell me. Not a support ticket. Me.
